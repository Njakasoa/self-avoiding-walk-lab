# CLAIM-0005 — Five finite singularity enclosures; infinite family conjectural

Candidate M3 target: the generating function J of irreducible NE-prudent ramps
is non-D-finite. Status: **OPEN; not accepted**. Publication priority UNRESOLVED.
M4 is conditional and has not begun.

The exact relation J=(P-H)/(1+P) and the meromorphic continuation of P,H are
from Bacher–Beaton, Weakly prudent self-avoiding bridges, FPSAC 2014. The known
common simple poles of P,H are removable for J. A new zero of 1+P is a
singularity of J if H differs from -1 there.

The local certificate encloses five such zeros in disjoint rational brackets
centered near 0.41257508666590646, 0.41381169333386277,
0.41403636149110697, 0.41411430354223307, 0.41415020038700697.
Each bracket has rational radius 10^-20. All sign checks, square-root rounding
and geometric series remainder bounds use integer/dyadic interval arithmetic.
The lower bound for 1+H over each whole bracket exceeds 0.36.

Scope: these are five finite singularity existence checks, conditional on the
primary formulas and continuation theorem as audited. They do not prove an
infinite family, non-D-finiteness, or the corresponding statement for weakly
prudent bridges W. See proofs/M3_PRUDENT_CANDIDATE.md and the independent formula
and Astra reviews. Source-frozen receipts are required before acceptance of the
finite computational checkpoint.

Next decisive mathematical obligation: prove existence of infinitely many
zeros of 1+P with 1+H nonzero, or find a counterexample to the proposed family.
An all-index interlacing lemma is supplied in proofs/M3_PRUDENT_INTERLACING.md.
The all-index noncancellation argument remains unproved.
