# Contribution review

Publication priority: **UNRESOLVED**. This is a publication candidate, not a
submission, external peer review, or claim of first discovery.

The theorem concerns the generating function J of irreducible NE-prudent
ramps under Bacher–Beaton's convention, with J=(P-H)/(1+P). The manuscript
proves that J has infinitely many real poles accumulating at sqrt(2)-1 and
therefore is non-D-finite. It does not prove the analogous assertion for the
further weakly prudent bridge quotient W, nor determine the connective
constant of unrestricted square-lattice self-avoiding walks.

The proposed contribution is the noncancellation argument. Exact signed-moment
identities regularize the h-zero before taking a critical limit. A Gamma
factorization controls the discrete crossing, its integrable exponent and
uniform tails. Certified integral signs then produce actual noncancelled
zeros in infinitely many disjoint phase intervals. Finite numerical agreement
is not substituted for these uniform estimates.

Bacher and Beaton (2014) provide the kernel formulas, the renewal identity and
meromorphic continuation; they explicitly leave non-D-finiteness of J open in
that article. Those results are inputs, not claimed as new. Generic Gamma
reflection/asymptotics, interval arithmetic and rectangle integration are
standard tools. Earlier automaton compression, weighted partitions and ladder
formulas in this repository are supporting experiments, not the contribution
of this manuscript.
