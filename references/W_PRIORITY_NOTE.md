The exact model is the two-sided weakly prudent bridge series W of
Bacher–Beaton. Their 2014 paper explicitly leaves its non-D-finiteness open
in Section 4.2, after giving the quotient W=I/(1-I):
[official paper](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/2445/pdf),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.46298/dmtcs.2445).

A further primary source is Nicholas Beaton's June 2015 CanaDAM talk,
*Solvable self-avoiding walk and polygon models with large growth rates*.
PDF page 102 (zero-based page 101) labels non-D-finiteness of the weakly
prudent bridge series as a Bacher–Beaton conjecture. The adjacent theorem
concerns coefficient growth, not this non-D-finiteness claim:
[author-hosted slides](https://www.nicholasbeaton.com/talks/CanaDAM_June_2015.pdf).
This distinction matters because a search-result excerpt can join the two
boxes and incorrectly suggest that non-D-finiteness was already a theorem.
