# Candidate selection after the NEXT sweeps

The sweeps generated three types of observation without selecting an expected
numerical answer: equitable state-count changes, cost/bound tradeoffs between
bridge dictionaries, and weighted improvements across a parameter family.

For selecting an object to *audit*, use the requested importance × possible
rigor × plausible novelty / certification-cost rule. The following ordinal
scores (1–5, cost in relative local units) are a transparent triage heuristic,
not probabilities or objective measurements. They were assigned after the
sweeps to allocate the independent follow-up; they are not preregistered data.

| Observation | Importance | Possible rigor | Initial novelty allowance | Cost | Score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Decreasing quotient size and source-class explanation | 2 | 5 | 1 | 1 | 10 |
| Better bridge lower bound at equal DFS budget | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 6 |
| Simultaneous weighted memory improvements | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 6 |

The first observation was therefore selected for a complete falsification cycle,
not promoted as a new theorem. It differs from merely printing the known parity
plateau: the machine detected fewer quotient classes at larger memory. The
interpretation predicts a quotient isomorphism and an unseen m12 state count.

Sequence performed:

1. E1 generated 207 classes at m9 and204 at m10.
2. Root proposed the line-graph/source-class explanation and wrote its proof.
3. The m11 quotient predicted q12=983−25=958 before the independent m12 build.
4. A separate implementation rebuilt geometry and refinement, checked the
   quotient isomorphism, and rejected even-memory/triangular overgeneralizations.
5. Sinks and reducibility were attacked separately; they cannot simply be deleted.
6. Primary-source priority was audited separately; standard line-graph,
   higher-block and equitable-partition ingredients constrain any novelty claim.
7. Astra reviewed mathematics, target scope and the complete NEXT contract.

See CLAIM-0003 for the exact statement and evidence. The resulting explanation
does not change a spectral radius or beat known bounds. After the priority
filter, no positive scientific novelty score is justified for any of these three
outputs. M2 is an operational discovery engine with automatic observations;
M3 as a novel scientific contribution and M4 publication remain UNRESOLVED.

The unsuccessful order≤5 bridge recurrence screens are retained. They only rule
out the prescribed full-start fits on the observed terms; they do not prove a
family has no rational generating function or no higher-order recurrence.
