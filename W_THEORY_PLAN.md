Status: PAUSED by the maintainer on 2026-09-12; incomplete. Baseline f925ac6 (M7), branch codex/w-complete-theory.

## Completion obligations derived from the requested research direction

1. Connect the accessible certified poles to the asymptotic family.
   Establish uniqueness and simplicity in the accessible bands and a
   coverage argument leaving no unexamined integer indices between them
   and the eventual theorem. The current starting evidence is N=32;
   six isolated brackets and a huge threshold are not continuous coverage.
2. Reduce the effective thresholds sufficiently to make that connection
   possible. A smaller but still inaccessible threshold is progress,
   not completion of this obligation.
3. Improve quantitative control of pole positions and residues, with
   proved remainders and clear effective domains. Keep asymptotic
   coefficients separate from finite certificates and usable error bounds.
4. Integrate the results with exact reproducible arithmetic, independent
   analytic review, source-frozen receipts and a requirement-by-requirement
   final audit. Preserve accepted M3–M7 sources and all diagnostic failures.

The goal is not complete while any obligation is unproved. No successful
finite scan or narrower milestone substitutes for the full goal.
